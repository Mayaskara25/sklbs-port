Semantic HTML (`header`, `nav`, `main`, `section`, `article`, `aside`, `footer`) organizes the résumé content. Class and attribute selectors handle filtering; `:hover`, `:focus-visible`, and `:active` show interaction states. `border-box`, consistent padding, margins, and gaps control spacing. Flexbox aligns navigation, projects, and actions; media queries stack them on smaller screens. Three.js renders interactive 3D scenes, with reduced-motion and WebGL fallbacks.
